Community Guidelines

NFD is a place to share first-hand experiences, good and bad, without turning someone else’s story into a target.

What belongs here

  • Honest accounts of your own experience, including difficult experiences and reports of misconduct.
  • Positive stories, lessons learned, and opinions clearly presented as your perspective.
  • Evidence you have the right to share, with unrelated private details removed.

What cannot be shared

  • Explicit sexual images or videos, non-consensual intimate images, or any sexual content involving minors.
  • Threats, targeted harassment, hate, or graphic sexual or violent material.
  • Private information that could put someone at risk, such as account credentials, home addresses, or private messages unrelated to the story.
  • Claims presented as independently verified facts when they are personal allegations or opinions. Describe what you experienced and what you know first-hand.

Automatic review

If an NFD administrator enables automatic review, story text and attached photos or videos are sent to Google Gemini for a guideline check before sharing. A failed check keeps the post from being shared and may suggest clearer, non-graphic wording. The suggestion is optional; the author remains in control of edits. Documents are not sent to Gemini for this check. NFD keeps the original file with the post and requests deletion of Gemini’s temporary review copy as soon as review finishes; Google says Files API uploads are automatically deleted after 48 hours. Google’s unpaid-service terms permit human review and use of submitted content to improve products, and Google advises against submitting sensitive, confidential, or personal information to that service. Read the Privacy page for details about third-party processing.

Automated checks can make mistakes. A review is a community-guideline check, not a finding that an allegation is true or false and not a guarantee of legal or government acceptance.
